# yolo26s_v9_1280

The component detector the robot runs: a small YOLO26 model, fine-tuned on our own
photos plus a public PCB dataset, at 1280-pixel resolution.

The name reads: YOLO26, size "s", version 9 of our Roboflow export, image size 1280.

## Model

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Architecture | YOLO26s (Ultralytics), about 9.5 million parameters, 20.8 GFLOPs fused |
| Starting weights | `yolo26s.pt`, pretrained on COCO |
| Classes | capacitor, resistor, transformer |
| Weights | `runs/yolo26s_v9_1280/weights/best.pt` |
| Copy used by the robot | `detection_model/weights/yolo26s_v9_1280.pt` |

## Dataset (`combined_v9`)

| Split | Images | Capacitors | Resistors | Transformers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Train, our photos | 107, each included three times | 2,020 | 51 | 153 |
| Train, public images | 1,296 | 4,832 | 1,154 | 2,547 |
| Validation, our photos | 30 | 762 | 7 | 52 |
| Test, our photos | 15 | 253 | 8 | 29 |

- **Our photos:** version 9 of the Roboflow project "carm capacitor test set"
  (<https://universe.roboflow.com/carm-yitb6/carm-capacitor-test-set/dataset/9>).
  152 photos, a mix of RealSense frames (1280x720) and phone photos, exported fit
  within 1280 pixels with no Roboflow augmentation.
- **Public images:** [PCB Component Detection](https://datasetninja.com/pcb-component-detection)
  from Dataset Ninja (CC0). Cap1-4 are mapped to capacitor, Resistor to resistor and
  Transformer to transformer. MOSFET and MOV labels are dropped.
- **Split:** by source photo, stratified so resistors land in validation and test. No
  photo is in more than one split. Our training photos are repeated three times so they
  are not drowned out by the public set.
- **Labels:** mostly polygons in Roboflow (3,013 polygons, 171 boxes), converted to
  bounding boxes by `prepare_data.py`.

## Training settings

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Image size | 1280 |
| Batch size | 8 |
| Epochs | up to 150; stopped at 104 by early stopping (patience 30) |
| Best epoch | 74 (saved as `best.pt`) |
| Optimizer | AdamW, learning rate 0.00143, chosen automatically by Ultralytics (`optimizer=auto`) |
| Mixed precision (AMP) | On |
| Seed | 0, deterministic |
| Training time | 55 minutes on an RTX 5070 (12 GB) |
| Software | Ultralytics 8.4.172, PyTorch 2.11.0 + CUDA 12.8, Python 3.10 |

Augmentation is the Ultralytics default: mosaic (off for the last 10 epochs),
horizontal flip with probability 0.5, random scale (0.5) and translation (0.1), HSV
colour jitter (0.015 / 0.7 / 0.4), RandAugment and random erasing (0.4). No rotation,
vertical flip, mixup or copy-paste.

Apart from image size, batch size, epoch limit, patience and seed, every setting is
the Ultralytics default.

### Reproduce

```bash
python3 prepare_data.py --ours carm_v9 --out combined_v9 --repeat 3
.venv/bin/python train.py --data combined_v9 --name yolo26s_v9_1280 --imgsz 1280 --batch 8
```

## Results

Test split of `combined_v9` (15 photos, 290 objects), inference size 1280:

| Class | Objects | Precision | Recall | mAP@50 | mAP@50-95 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All | 290 | 0.78 | 0.85 | 0.84 | 0.60 |
| Capacitor | 253 | 0.85 | 0.88 | 0.91 | 0.70 |
| Resistor | 8 | 0.76 | 0.88 | 0.81 | 0.49 |
| Transformer | 29 | 0.74 | 0.78 | 0.80 | 0.60 |

Validation (30 photos, 821 objects): mAP@50 0.85, mAP@50-95 0.63.

Precision and recall are reported at the confidence where F1 is highest, about 0.50,
which is also the threshold the robot uses. Counting detections at that threshold on the
test photos:

| | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Labelled parts reported with the right class | 254 of 290 |
| Reported with the wrong class | 1 (a capacitor called resistor) |
| Not reported | 35 |
| Detections with no label under them | 47 |

The plots (test metrics, training curves, precision-recall and confidence curves,
confusion matrix, dataset) and the numbers behind them are in
[`../results/yolo26s_v9_1280/`](../results/yolo26s_v9_1280/README.md).

## Use on the robot

`detection_model/yolo_model.py` feeds the model the 1920x1080 colour frame of the
RealSense, with the camera's sharpness set to 100, scaled down to image size 1280
(`MODEL_IMGSZ`). It keeps detections with confidence 0.50 or higher (`CONF_THRESHOLD`)
and treats two detections within 8 mm of each other as one part.

In the five pick-and-place trials of 2026-10-11 (ten loose capacitors on the board each,
record in [`../results/five_trials_2026-10-11/`](../results/five_trials_2026-10-11/README.md)):

- Every one of the 50 capacitors was detected in the first image of its trial, and in
  none of the 55 detection passes were there fewer detections than parts on the board.
- The first images held 53 detections for 50 parts: three were a second box on a part
  that already had one, and one of those was labelled transformer.
- Across all passes, four boxes carried the wrong class (two transformer, two resistor).
  None led to a pick: the transformer boxes were dropped as second boxes on a capacitor,
  and no part was ever picked as a resistor.

## Limitations

- **The labels are incomplete and loose.** A review found 115 real parts missing from
  the labels of our 152 photos, and many boxes that are larger than the part or offset
  from it. Some of the 47 unlabelled detections above are therefore correct, and the
  scores understate the model somewhat.
- **The test photos show the same boards as the training photos,** so the scores say how
  well the model knows these boards, not how it does on a new one. On two boards it had
  never seen, it missed several transformers and every loose resistor.
- **The resistor scores rest on 8 test objects,** so they swing a lot.
- **Typical false positives:** TO-220 transistors on heatsinks, upright MOSFETs,
  shielded inductors, push-buttons and pin headers, all reported as capacitors.
- **The camera setting on the robot is not the one it was trained on.** Training used
  1280x720 RealSense frames and phone photos; the robot runs it on 1920x1080 frames at
  sharpness 100. It has only been checked there on loose capacitors, in the trials above.
- **Double boxes** on a single part occur, sometimes with different classes. Which class
  the part gets is decided by the higher confidence.
