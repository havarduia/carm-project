# yolo26s_v9_1280

The component detector the robot currently runs: a small YOLO26 model, fine-tuned on
our own photos plus a public PCB dataset, at 1280-pixel resolution.

The name reads: YOLO26, size "s", version 9 of our Roboflow export, image size 1280.

## Model

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Architecture | YOLO26s (Ultralytics), about 9.5 million parameters, 20.8 GFLOPs fused |
| Starting weights | `yolo26s.pt`, pretrained on COCO |
| Classes | capacitor, resistor, transformer |
| Weights | `runs/yolo26s_v9_1280/weights/best.pt` |
| Copy used by the robot | `detection_model/weights/yolo26s_v9_1280.pt` |

## Dataset (`combined_v9`)

| Split | Images | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Train | 1,617 | 1,296 public PCB images + 107 of our photos, each included three times (321 files) |
| Validation | 30 | Our photos only |
| Test | 15 | Our photos only |

- **Our photos:** version 9 of the Roboflow project "carm capacitor test set"
  (<https://universe.roboflow.com/carm-yitb6/carm-capacitor-test-set/dataset/9>).
  152 photos, a mix of RealSense frames (1280x720) and phone photos, exported fit
  within 1280 pixels with no Roboflow augmentation.
- **Public images:** [PCB Component Detection](https://datasetninja.com/pcb-component-detection)
  from Dataset Ninja (CC0). Cap1-4 are mapped to capacitor, Resistor to resistor and
  Transformer to transformer. MOSFET and MOV labels are dropped.
- **Split:** by source photo, stratified so resistors land in validation and test. No
  validation or test photo is also in training. Our training photos are repeated three
  times so they are not drowned out by the public set.
- **Labels:** mostly polygons in Roboflow (3,013 polygons, 171 boxes), converted to
  bounding boxes by `prepare_data.py`.

Test-set objects: 253 capacitors, 8 resistors, 29 transformers (290 in total).

## Training settings

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Image size | 1280 |
| Batch size | 8 |
| Epochs | up to 150; stopped at 104 by early stopping (patience 30) |
| Best epoch | 74 (saved as `best.pt`) |
| Optimizer | AdamW, learning rate 0.00143, chosen automatically by Ultralytics (`optimizer=auto`) |
| Mixed precision (AMP) | On |
| Seed | 0, deterministic |
| Training time | 55 minutes on an RTX 5070 (12 GB) |
| Software | Ultralytics 8.4.172, PyTorch 2.11.0 + CUDA 12.8, Python 3.10 |

Augmentation is the Ultralytics default: mosaic (off for the last 10 epochs),
horizontal flip with probability 0.5, random scale (0.5) and translation (0.1), HSV
colour jitter (0.015 / 0.7 / 0.4), RandAugment and random erasing (0.4). No rotation,
vertical flip, mixup or copy-paste.

Apart from image size, batch size, epoch limit, patience and seed, every setting is
the Ultralytics default.

### Reproduce

```bash
python3 prepare_data.py --ours carm_v9 --out combined_v9 --repeat 3
.venv/bin/python train.py --data combined_v9 --name yolo26s_v9_1280 --imgsz 1280 --batch 8
```

## Results

Test split of `combined_v9` (15 photos, 290 objects), inference size 1280:

| Class | Objects | Precision | Recall | mAP@50 | mAP@50-95 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All | 290 | 0.78 | 0.85 | 0.84 | 0.60 |
| Capacitor | 253 | 0.85 | 0.88 | 0.91 | 0.70 |
| Resistor | 8 | 0.76 | 0.88 | 0.81 | 0.49 |
| Transformer | 29 | 0.74 | 0.78 | 0.80 | 0.60 |

Validation (30 photos, 821 objects): mAP@50 0.85, mAP@50-95 0.63.

Precision and recall are reported at the confidence where F1 is highest, about 0.50.

The KPI plot is `runs/yolo26s_v9_1280/kpi_test.png`; regenerate it with
`.venv/bin/python plot_kpi.py --name yolo26s_v9_1280`.

## Use on the robot

`detection_model/yolo_model.py` runs the model at image size 1280 (`MODEL_IMGSZ`) and
keeps detections with confidence 0.50 or higher (`CONF_THRESHOLD`).

## Known limitations (found 2026-10-10)

- **Labels are incomplete and loose.** A review found 115 real parts missing from the
  labels of our 152 photos, and many boxes that are larger than the part or offset
  from it. Scored against labels with those 115 parts added, the same weights reach
  precision 0.88, recall 0.81, mAP@50 0.88 and mAP@50-95 0.65 on the test photos.
- **Test photos show the same boards as the training photos,** so the scores say how
  well the model knows these boards, not how it does on a new one. On two unseen
  boards it missed several transformers and every loose resistor.
- **Resistor scores rest on 8 test objects,** so they swing a lot.
- **Typical false positives:** TO-220 transistors on heatsinks, upright MOSFETs,
  shielded inductors, push-buttons and pin headers, all reported as capacitors.
- **Trained on 1280x720 RealSense frames and phone photos.** It has not been trained
  on the 1920x1080, sharpness-100 camera setting chosen for the new dataset.
